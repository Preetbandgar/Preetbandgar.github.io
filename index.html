About Me

I'm a robotics software engineer passionate about designing intelligent systems that solve real-world problems. With a Master's in Automation, Control & Robotics from Sheffield Hallam University, UK, I've built a strong foundation in autonomous systems, ROS2, SLAM, Nav2, and cloud robotics, grounded in hands-on experimentation and real-world deployment.

My work in computer vision and autonomous navigation has involved developing algorithms that enable machines to perceive and interpret their surroundings, from SLAM-based mapping to autonomous path planning. I believe that robust localization and navigation are at the heart of intelligent robotics behavior.

I thrive at the intersection of robotics and cloud infrastructure, where ROS2 systems meet modern DevOps practices. My experience has shaped my ability to think both analytically and creatively, translating complex robotics concepts into scalable, reliable cloud-connected systems.

I'm driven by the opportunity to learn, to collaborate with purpose-driven teams, and to build technologies that make a tangible impact. To me, great engineering is about more than just precision—it's about curiosity, persistence, and a deep understanding of how robotic systems behave in the real world.

At my core, I enjoy solving hard problems, building systems that navigate and see, and continuously growing through each project, conversation, and challenge I take on.
