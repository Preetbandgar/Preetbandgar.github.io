A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From mechanical systems
to robot software.

I work with ROS 2 and Python to build, test and debug mobile robot software. I like the practical side of robotics: tracing a topic that isn't publishing, checking TF frames, finding out why a controller stalls, and making a simulation behave as expected.

I completed a Master’s in Automation Control & Robotics at Sheffield Hallam University, UK (now offered as MSc Robotics). Before that, I studied Mechanical Engineering at Savitribai Phule Pune University. That background helps me connect the physical behaviour of robots with the software controlling them.

My work includes Gazebo robot models, Nav2 mapping and navigation, ROS 2 applications in rclpy, and development workflows using Docker and GitHub Actions.

Vision-Based Real-Time Respiration Rate Monitoring

Developed a MATLAB-based method to estimate breathing rate from chest movement captured by a laptop camera. Used Lucas–Kanade optical flow and compared readings with manually counted breaths from six participants.
