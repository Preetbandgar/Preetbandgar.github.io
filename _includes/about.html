A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From mechanical systems
to robot software.

I develop robotics software with ROS 2 and Python, mainly for mobile robots. My work includes autonomous navigation with Nav2, SLAM-based mapping, Gazebo simulation, TF and sensor integration. I also use Docker and GitHub Actions to make development and testing more repeatable.

I enjoy the engineering work behind a successful demo: tracing missing ROS 2 messages, checking coordinate frames, investigating navigation failures and understanding why a robot behaves differently from what I expected. My projects show those decisions, the tests I ran and the results I could verify.

I have a Master's in Robotics from Sheffield Hallam University, United Kingdom and a background in mechanical engineering. I’m now building a physical differential-drive robot with ESP32 and micro-ROS to move from simulation into embedded control and hardware integration.
